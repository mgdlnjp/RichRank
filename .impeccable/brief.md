# RichRank: main screen

Scope: the single calculator screen (App.js). Visitor mode: Operate (enter a salary, read the result), fun register per PRODUCT.md.
Job: type an amount, pick frequency and currency, instantly see annual income, years to 1 M and 1 Md, and a rank. Constraints: gross estimate, stated on screen; no invented statistics.
Memorable moment: the rank card flipping/landing with its rarity when the number changes tier.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Your salary becomes a collectible card with a rarity, not a figure in a form. Refuses the category default of a white calculator with a green result card.
OWN-WORLD: Trading-card world. Lavender-white ground, deep ink navy, electric violet as the one tint for controls, pink and sunflower as rarity foils. Thick ink outlines, chunky rounded cards, stat pills, a holographic foil frame only on the rank card. One display face with character (Bricolage Grotesque) for numbers and titles; system font for small labels.
STORY: The visitor sees their pay turned into a card, laughs at their rank name, understands the years-to-million gap, tries another number or shares.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Top: wordmark + logo. Then a large amount field with a €/$ segmented control and a Jour/Semaine/Mois segmented control. Below: the rank card (rarity badge, rank name, emoji art, three stat pills: per year, 1 M in, 1 Md in). Live update as you type, no "Calculer" button. Estimate disclaimer under the card. Share action on the card.
FORM: Collectible trading card, impeccable's pick (my list position 1). seed:b0c1be1f7b3460ee
SIGNATURE: When the tier changes the card flips once (Y rotation) and its rarity foil changes; within a tier the numbers just update. Reduce Motion: crossfade.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
