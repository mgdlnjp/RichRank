# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

ios

(Expo / React Native: the same code also ships to Android and runs on the web for previews. Design language is one shared system, not per-OS.)

## Users

Curious people, mostly young adults, who want a quick, funny reality check on their pay: "how far am I from being a millionaire?". Casual, one-handed use on a phone, often shared with friends.

## Product Purpose

RichRank turns a salary (daily, weekly or monthly) into an annual income, then tells the user how many years it would take to earn 1 million and 1 billion, and gives them a playful rank. Success: the result is understood instantly, makes the user smile, and makes them want to try another number or show a friend.

## Positioning

Not a finance tool: a toy built on real arithmetic. The rank ladder and "years to a million / a billion" turn a dull number into a joke you can share.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Inputs: amount, frequency (daily = 260 working days, weekly = 52, monthly = 12), currency (€ or $, user's choice).
- Outputs: annual income, years to 1 M, years to 1 Md, a playful rank tier.
- Arithmetic is gross pay only: no tax, no savings, no investment returns. Results are estimates and must say so.
- Single screen app, no account, no network, no stored data.
- French UI.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: RichRank.
- Tone: fun and colourful, playful, game-like (confirmed by the user, 2026-11-17).
- A new logo / app icon is part of the redesign (old icon is the Expo placeholder).

## Evidence on Hand

No real users, testimonials or statistics. Do not invent wealth statistics or "richer than X % of the world" claims.

## Product Principles

1. The number is the hero: one input, one big answer.
2. Funny, never mean: ranks tease kindly, whatever the salary.
3. Honest maths: always clear that it is a gross, simplified estimate.
4. Instant: no extra steps between typing and seeing the result.
